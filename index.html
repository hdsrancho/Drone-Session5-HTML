Session 5

Drone Parts and Assembly

What every part is and what it does

Parts of a Drone

1 Frame

  • The skeleton of the drone
  • Arms carry the motors, the centre plate carries the board and battery
  • Must be light but stiff: carbon fibre, plastic or foam in DIY builds
  • Arm length decides the largest propeller you can fit

2 Motors (coreless)

  • Turns electric power into spin
  • Coreless: hollow coil cup with no iron core, so it is tiny, light and reacts fast
  • Brushed type with just two wires, driven straight from the board
  • DIY drones use these instead of BLDC motors
  • 4 motors: 2 clockwise, 2 anticlockwise

3 Propellers: Blade Shape

  • A propeller is a small rotating wing: thin and flat
  • Rounded leading edge cuts into the air, sharp trailing edge lets it leave smoothly
  • Narrow at the root, wide at the tip: the tip moves fastest, so it does the most work
  • The blade is twisted: steeper near the hub, flatter at the tip, so lift is even along it
  • The hub hole grips the motor shaft

3 Propellers: Making Lift

  • As it spins, the blade is tilted (pitched) against the air
  • It bites the air and throws it downward
  • The air pushes back up on the blade, and that is lift
  • Bigger or steeper blades give more lift but need more power

3 Propellers: CW and CCW

  • A spinning propeller twists the drone the opposite way
  • Two props turn clockwise, two anticlockwise, in opposite pairs, so the twists cancel
  • Mount each prop on its matching motor with the leading edge facing the spin and the curved face up
  • Speeding up one pair and slowing the other turns the drone (yaw)

4 Flight Controller

  • The brain: reads the gyro and the pilot, then sets each motor speed
  • ESC is on the same board: its motor drivers power the motors directly
  • No separate ESC modules, so fewer wires and less weight
  • Gyro and accelerometer built in keep it level
  • No GPS: the pilot flies by sight

5 Battery

  • Stores the energy for the whole drone
  • LiPo cells are light and give high current
  • Capacity in mAh sets flight time, more mAh means more weight
  • Connect to the board with correct polarity: red to positive (+), black to negative (-)
  • Never over-discharge, charge with a proper charger

6 Radio Link

  • Transmitter in the pilot’s hands: sticks send throttle, yaw, pitch and roll
  • Receiver on the drone listens and passes commands to the board
  • On many DIY drones the receiver is built into the board
  • Without GPS, keep the drone in sight and in range

How the Parts Work Together

  • Pilot command and battery power go in. The board sets motor speeds, motors spin props, gyro feedback corrects it.

Assembly Steps

1Fix the motors to the frame arms
2Mount the flight controller on the centre plate
3Connect motor wires to M1 to M4 pads
4Attach the battery and secure it
5Fit propellers last, matching CW and CCW
Note: the ESC and the radio receiver are already built into the flight controller board, so there is nothing separate to mount or wire. Only the motors and the battery connect to the board.

Before You Fly

  • Motor order and spin direction match the diagram
  • Props are on the correct motors and tight
  • Battery is secure and balanced in the centre
  • Wires clear of the propellers
  • Test the controls with props off first
